How it works

01
📤

Upload

Your audio file is sent to the FastAPI backend, validated, and given a unique job ID.

02
🔄

Convert

Any format (MP3, M4A, OGG…) is normalised to 16 kHz mono WAV via FFmpeg for Whisper compatibility.

03
🧠

Transcribe

OpenAI Whisper runs inference. Long audio is split into overlapping chunks to stay memory-efficient.

04
📋

Results

Full text, auto-detected language, and per-segment timestamps are returned and displayed here.
